Where You'll Stay

Accommodation is shared, guesthouse-style housing at Faraja House and Mary's compound on the Positive Life Kenya compound. Every attendee is paired with a roommate in a simple, clean twin or triple room with proper beds, bedding and mosquito nets.

Bathrooms are shared and kept clean, with running water available. The compound is gated and secured, with staff on-site around the clock, and communal lounge and dining areas where the group gathers between sessions and shares meals together. Wi-Fi is available across the compound, and all ground transportation — airport pickup, transfers and summit travel — is arranged for you.

$55 USD per day

Your summit fee covers shared accommodation and three meals a day, for the full stay.

What's Included

  • Shared accommodation for all 11 nights, with a paired roommate
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner daily — shared meals together

  • Ground transportation, including airport pickup and all summit travel
